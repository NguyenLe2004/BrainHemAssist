weights is granted here. Full weight provenance, contributor rights, training
data permissions and rights to distribute architecture-derived portions have

The underlying baseline implementation was compared with concrete upstream
source snapshots, not just paper titles. DE-UNeXt at commit
c70032d24e848d51a08fc2e569bac73c721932ba contains a nested MIT notice and
matching architecture/helper structure. Attention helpers on the export path
also match methods in Jongchan/attention-module at commit
459efad0e05ee7dde50c41ca10a3d0800bc3792a. UNeXt and SegFormer patch embedding
methods share equivalent source operations. These observations are source-
lineage evidence; the original local import revisions and complete chain of
permissions have not been established.
