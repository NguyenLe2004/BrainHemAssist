4. Baseline S artifacts and source-lineage references
Baseline S is included as models/baseline_s.onnx (FP32) and the derived
models/baseline_s.int8.onnx (selective MatMul INT8, other blocks FP32).
scripts/quantize_model.py reproduces the latter from the former. The project
owner publishes both artifacts without an open-source license grant for the
original weights; conversion adds no new permissions. Both graphs and learned
weights are inspectable. Applicable third-party terms remain with any covered
portions.

Source-lineage references include DE-UNeXt, whose snapshot at commit
c70032d24e848d51a08fc2e569bac73c721932ba contains a nested MIT notice and
matching architecture/helper structure. Attention helpers on the export path
also match methods in Jongchan/attention-module at commit
459efad0e05ee7dde50c41ca10a3d0800bc3792a. UNeXt and SegFormer patch embedding
methods share equivalent source operations. These observations are source-
lineage leads; the historical local import revisions remain unconfirmed.
